# Problem statement

Write the problem before the solution. A good statement is specific enough to be proven wrong.

## Template (PT-BR output)
> **[Quem]** precisa **[fazer o quê / alcançar qual resultado]** quando **[contexto/situação]**, mas hoje **[obstáculo]**, o que causa **[consequência mensurável: tempo, dinheiro, erro, risco]**.

## Checklist
- Who: a specific role or segment, not "users" or "companies".
- Context: when and where the problem happens.
- Obstacle: what blocks them today (including current workaround).
- Consequence: a number or a range, with source or marked **PREMISSA**.
- No solution words (app, dashboard, sensor) in the statement.

## How to test it
List 3 pieces of evidence that would confirm it and 3 that would refute it. Search for both.
